We audit a chest-radiograph report-generation pilot that did not reach its
intended benchmark and separate its administrative, statistical, and
instrumental obstacles. The benchmark is contact-gated: the project recorded
\NLocks{} access locks, comprising \NPermissionLocks{} permissions,
\NArtifactLocks{} evaluation artifact that arrives with the first permission,
and \NSubstitutionLocks{} self-imposed refusals to score a local corpus under
the benchmark's name. The permission requests were never submitted, the record
predates compute provisioning, and computation removes none of the locks.

The statistical limits are separate. For \NExceedSplit{} of \NFindings{}
labelled findings, a draw would need more reports than the frozen
\NTest{}-report split before it is expected to contain \MinPresent{} positives.
The pre-registered draw of \NCohort{} reports contains \SkewObserved{}
\SkewFinding{} positives against \SkewExpected{} expected (exact two-sided
probability \SkewP{}, threshold \Bonferroni{}); \NScored{} of \NFindings{}
observations clear the presence gate and \NUnderpowered{} are reported as
underpowered without a rate. On the scored cells the constant report misses
every gold positive and the retrieval control misses \RetMissedOne{} and
\RetMissedTwo{}, with one-sided 90\% upper limits \RetUpperOne{} and
\RetUpperTwo{}. The automatic labeller reads the constant report as
\MajAsserted{} on all \NCohort{} rows and never as the no-finding
observation, so the majority floor is the labeller's reading of one string;
surface-text F1 of \SurfaceRetrievalF{} and \SurfaceMajorityF{} carries none
of it.

The contributions are the obstacle inventory, the registered draw with
expected counts, and the labeller-reading result.
